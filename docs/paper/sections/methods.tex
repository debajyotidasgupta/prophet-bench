\section{The PROPHET marketplace}\label{sec:method}

\paragraph{Setup.}
A benchmark cycle consists of a procedurally generated task suite
$\mathcal{T}$ and an agent $\mathcal{A}$. Each task
$t \in \mathcal{T}$ is drawn from one of twelve families
(Section~\ref{sec:families}) with a known reference-panel difficulty
$\delta(t) \in [0, 1]$. Difficulty climbs from T0 (trivial) to T7
(``standard hardest''); we publish an additional
T\textsubscript{extreme} tier at $\delta \in [0.97, 1.0]$ calibrated
against 2025--2026 hard-task ceilings. For each $t$ the engine forms a
deterministic market offer
$(V_t, C_t, \kappa_t, \delta^{\mathrm{pass}}_t)$ from a family-specific
base plus hash-derived per-instance noise $\le 2\%$.

\paragraph{Actions and payoffs.}
The agent selects $a \in \{\textsc{Take}, \textsc{Quote},
\textsc{Pass}\}$, declares $\Phat \in [0, 1]$, and receives:
\textsc{Take}: $V_t y - C_t (1-y)$;
\textsc{Quote}: $V_t y - C_t (1-y) + \kappa_t (1 - 4(\Phat - y)^2)$;
\textsc{Pass}: $-\delta^{\mathrm{pass}}_t$.
Here $y \in \{0, 1\}$ is the mechanical verifier's outcome.
$\kappa(1 - 4(\Phat - y)^2)$ is a Brier-based proper scoring rule
scaled to $[-3\kappa, +\kappa]$: conditional on \textsc{Quote},
expected payoff is maximised iff
$\Phat = \mathbb{E}[y \mid t]$~\citep{brier1950,gneiting2007}. The
factor $4$ makes uniform $\Phat = 0.5$ yield zero calibration payoff,
eliminating the always-hedge strategy. The asymmetric
$[-3\kappa, +\kappa]$ band reflects production reality: over-confidence
drives bad decisions; caution costs research effort. The full
action-selection equilibrium is in Appendix~\ref{app:reproduce}.

\paragraph{Headline metrics and Pareto leaderboard.}
We report net payoff $\mathcal{P} = \sum_t \mathrm{payoff}(t)$, ECE
($10$ equal-width bins; BCa bootstrap $95\,\%$ CI as ECE is
non-differentiable, $B = 10^4$), Brier, log-loss, abstention precision
(on a $\rho = 0.10$ shadow sub-sample), and Model Overreach Point
$\MOP$ (the difficulty bin where stated $\Phat$ exceeds empirical
accuracy by $\ge 2\sigma$). We publish the Pareto frontier of
$(\mathcal{P}, \ECE)$ with a secondary $(\text{cost}, \mathcal{P})$
frontier; the frontier table, not any scalar, is the headline.

\paragraph{Statistical protocol.}\label{sec:stats}
Pairwise contrasts: paired Student's $t$ when $n \ge 30$ (else
Wilcoxon); McNemar's exact for binary correctness; permutation test for
$\Delta\ECE$ ($B = 10^4$). Effect sizes accompany every $p$ (Cohen's
$d$, rank-biserial $r$, paired odds-ratio, $|\Delta\ECE|$).
Holm--Bonferroni is applied \emph{within} each metric family
($\binom{16}{2} = 120$ tests for net-payoff; $120$ for ECE), not
across metrics. Raw and adjusted $p$-values are reported in parallel;
post-hoc power and MDE per headline contrast are in
Appendix~\ref{app:ablations}.

\paragraph{Per-agent $N$ and min-$N$ filter.}
The Standard tier targets $240$ tasks per agent; realised $N$ ranges
$55$--$292$ (multi-seed pooling on Pareto-essentials, provider-side
refusals). T\textsubscript{extreme} targets $40$ and realises
$28$--$100$. The pre-registered min-$N$ filter (Standard $N \ge 100$,
Ext $N \ge 20$) governs Pareto-flag and Spearman; rows below threshold
are displayed for transparency without the $\star$ marker.

\paragraph{Gaming resistance.}\label{sec:gaming}
We adopt the Berkeley RDI April~2026 attack taxonomy as a
pre-publication red-team checklist (Appendix~\ref{app:audit}). Key
mechanisms: procedural seeds with rotation (no fixed eval set),
mechanical primary metrics (no LLM-judge), sandboxed code-family
verifiers (\texttt{subprocess.run} with $5$\,s timeout, restricted
PATH), and the Pareto frontier itself --- single-axis gaming collapses
an orthogonal axis.
