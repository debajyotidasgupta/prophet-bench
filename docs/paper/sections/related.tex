\section{Related work}\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Saturation of static benchmarks.}
By May 2026, MMLU-Pro and GPQA-Diamond cluster above $94\,\%$;
SWE-bench Verified at $\approx 94\,\%$~\cite{swebench};
HLE rose from $8\,\%$ to $\approx 65\,\%$ in 16 months~\cite{hle};
ARC-AGI-2 from $53\,\%$ to $85\,\%$ in four months~\cite{arcagi2}.
The unsaturated axes are all calibrated, persistent agency:
ARC-AGI-3 $<1\,\%$~\cite{arcagi3}, SWE-EVO $\approx 25\,\%$,
MemoryAgentBench selective-forgetting $<30\,\%$~\cite{memoryagentbench}.

\paragraph{Verbal calibration and faithfulness.}
Kadavath et~al.~\cite{kadavath2022} showed LLMs have non-trivial verbal
calibration. Recent faithfulness work~\cite{anthropic_faithfulness}
found unfaithful chains-of-thought are systematically \emph{longer}
than faithful ones and that models verbalise decision-relevant hints
in only $25$--$39\,\%$ of cases --- verbose confidence decouples from
competence. PROPHET pays only when stated $\Phat$ matches outcome rates.

\paragraph{Proper scoring rules.} Brier (1950) and Gneiting--Raftery
\cite{gneiting2007} provide the theory; Guo et~al.~\cite{guo2017}
established ECE and temperature scaling for deep classifiers. We adopt
Brier because it is bounded, smooth, and admits closed-form expected
payoff at known $P[y=1\!\mid\!t]$ --- log-score blows up at extremes
and is gameable under borderline tasks.

\paragraph{Agent benchmarks and gaming.}
GAIA~\cite{gaia}, AgentBench, MMAU, and OSWorld~\cite{osworld}
established multi-step tool-use surfaces. The Berkeley RDI April~2026
audit~\cite{berkeley_rdi_audit} demonstrated trivial exploits giving
$100\,\%$ on eight major agent benchmarks; we adopt their attack
taxonomy as a pre-publication red-team checklist
(Section~\ref{sec:gaming}).

\paragraph{Concurrent calibration-with-skin-in-the-game work.}
\textbf{Prophet Arena}~\cite{prophet_arena} (UChicago, Oct.~2025)
evaluates frontier LLMs on live Kalshi prediction-market events with
Brier and a market-return metric; it operates on \emph{live external
events} rather than procedural tasks with mechanical verifiers, has no
abstention action, and admits no Pareto comparison. \textbf{KalshiBench},
\textbf{PolyBench}, and \textbf{ForecastBench}~\cite{forecastbench}
cover similar territory and inherit the same external-event dependency.
\textbf{BAS}~\cite{bas_oxford} (Oxford, Apr.~2026) is the closest
sibling: an asymmetric-overconfidence proper-scoring rule with
abstention. PROPHET differs in (i) a bounded Brier-based payoff
$[-3\kappa, +\kappa]$ rather than an unbounded log-style penalty,
(ii) an explicit \textsc{Take}/\textsc{Quote}/\textsc{Pass} action menu,
(iii) a procedurally generated 12-family suite with mechanical
verifiers, and (iv) a two-dimensional Pareto frontier rather than a
scalar. \textbf{AbstentionBench}~\cite{abstentionbench} and
\textbf{Going-All-In}~\cite{going_all_in} jointly motivate the bundle:
the former shows current systems do not abstain when ECE indicates they
should; the latter that prompted-confidence elicitation diverges from
behavioural commitment. \textbf{CRS}~\cite{crs_composite} proposes a
single composite score; we deliberately avoid this in favour of Pareto.

\paragraph{Hard procedural task families.}
\textbf{Enigmata}~\cite{enigmata} ships 36 puzzle generators with
Python verifiers; Sequential-Hard holds o4-mini at $34\,\%$.
\textbf{ZebraLogic}~\cite{zebralogic} documents a ``curse of complexity''
dropping Claude~3.5 to $12.4\,\%$ on the hard tier.
\textbf{MultiZebraLogic}~\cite{multizebralogic} shows red-herring
clauses drop o3-mini by $15\pm 7$\,pp. \textbf{NPPC}~\cite{nppc} ships
25 NP-complete generators where all frontier models fall below $20\,\%$
past complexity 5. \textbf{OJBench}~\cite{ojbench},
\textbf{LiveCodeBench-Pro}~\cite{lcbpro}, and
\textbf{AetherCode}~\cite{aethercode} sit at $0$--$10\,\%$ pass@1 on
their hard tiers; \textbf{NoLiMa}~\cite{nolima} shows GPT-4o falling
from $99.3\,\%$ to $69.7\,\%$ under minimally lexical needle-in-haystack.
PROPHET's T\textsubscript{extreme} tier draws on the same recipe so
accuracy spread is preserved at $\sim$10--40\,\% after the standard
tier saturates. The shared word with Prophet Arena reflects only the
proper-scoring-rule lineage; the design choices are non-overlapping.
